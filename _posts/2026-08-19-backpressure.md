# AI software development and backpressure

I've been a professional software engineer for 30 years, and AI has changed
things a great deal in the last year or two.

One big thing is that we can now produce code that's
_probably_ fine, but has to be reviewed and tested, faster than we can
do that reviewing and testing. How do you deal with that?

My favourite LLM and I wrote an essay together, with animated diagrams, even,
explaining how one of the solutions to this is _backpressure_.

Or in plain language, _slow down and stop swamping me_.
